## The line

> My toaster has two settings: raw bread and a small house fire. There is no in-between, and it knows it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

It lands on a cold read: two settings, raw bread and a small house fire, is a small domestic tragedy everyone with a cheap toaster recognises at once. The turn is the last clause — there is no in-between, and it knows it — which gives the toaster intent, as if the appliance chose this and is proud of it. The escalation from breakfast to arson in one breath is the whole joke, and the second sentence is the kicker that makes it a line rather than a complaint. Two sentences, nothing left to trim, no setup notes and no explanation. One line, 103 characters, well inside the 200-character gate.
